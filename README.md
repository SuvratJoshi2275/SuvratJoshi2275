## About Me

I'm a **B.Tech Computer Science Engineering (Hons.) — Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning** student at the **University of Petroleum & Energy Studies (UPES)**.

My primary interest lies in developing machine learning systems that extend beyond model training into **research-backed, deployable applications**.

I work across the complete ML lifecycle — dataset preparation, representation learning, experimentation, model evaluation, inference integration, and interactive application development.

My interests include:

- **Deep Learning & Neural Architectures** — Representation learning, transformer adaptation, and model optimization.
- **Natural Language Processing** — Contextual embeddings, multilingual understanding, semantic retrieval, and language models.
- **Graph Learning & Football Intelligence** — Tactical relationships, passing networks, and graph-based modeling.
- **Recommendation Systems** — Collaborative signals, semantic similarity, and personalized ranking.
- **Computer Vision** — Transfer learning, visual classification, and applied image understanding.
- **Applied AI Research** — Reproducible experiments, rigorous evaluation, and full-stack ML integration.

Currently developing **SquadSync** and **Contexta**, two research-oriented systems exploring football intelligence and multilingual language understanding.

### 02 / Contexta

**Context-Aware Multilingual Intelligence**

An integrated multilingual NLP research platform combining **hybrid word sense disambiguation, Hindi–English code-mixed language identification, and experimental neural machine translation**.

Features a Spotify-inspired **React + TypeScript research interface** connected to a FastAPI inference backend.

The system integrates XLM-RoBERTa LoRA, interpretable lexical rules, character-level TF-IDF classification, and fine-tuned MarianMT.

**Technical Focus**

`XLM-RoBERTa` `LoRA` `MarianMT` `React` `TypeScript` `FastAPI`

**Research:** 20 notebooks · 35 ambiguous lemmas · 70 curated senses.

**Platform:** 12 interactive research modules · 3 NLP tasks.
